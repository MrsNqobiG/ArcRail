Raayl × Arc · settlement rail

What we've built so far

The goal: a customer sends a payment in rand or in USDC, Raayl converts it where needed, moves it over Circle's Arc network, and pays the receiver into a bank account or a USDC wallet. This sheet shows the parts of that system that are built today and how they connect to Raayl's existing systems.
