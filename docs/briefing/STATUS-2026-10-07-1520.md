# Arc Rail: status at 15:20, 7 October 2026

Arc testnet only. Nothing touches Arc mainnet, real money or the production systems of DFNS, Circle or VALR.

## In one line

The core of the Arc payment rail is built. Most of it has passed or is close to passing independent review. The full payment journey (pay in, convert, send, pay out, report to the client) should be built and fully reviewed at about 16:15–16:45 today.

## What you can see today

- **Code on GitHub:** branch `co1-v3/nova-arc-d1` in the ArcRail repository, with a commit at about 15:20. Every commit is scanned for leaked secrets first.
- **Build map:** the schematic of what has been built (Arc-Rail-Build-Map.pdf).
- **Payment demo (runs, 2 of 2 checks pass):** a USDC payment on Arc testnet goes through seven steps: created, funds reserved, approved, submitted, broadcast, confirmed, network fee posted. At each step the real ledger, status and client-history parts record it. The ledger entries balance, every step is in the client history in order, and a repeated entry is never recorded twice. A second run shows a rejected approval ending as FAILED, with the failure in the history. The DFNS and Arc network steps are simulated, because the payment-flow part that connects them is still being built. This is a demo on parts still in review, so it is not evidence for sign-off. Run it with `npx vitest run -c demo/vitest.demo.config.ts`.

## Status of each part

"Verified" means an independent reviewer found no blocking issues. A blocking issue is anything that could lose, double-count or misdirect money, or skip a required control.

| Part | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Arc connection | Watches the Arc network and confirms each transfer from the chain itself | **Verified** |
| Quote | Prices a payment end to end: conversion, network fee and payout fee | **Verified**; being updated to Raayl's 5-minute pricing-code process |
| DFNS safety gate | The only way a transfer reaches DFNS custody: checks amount, recipient and network before anything is signed | One blocking issue left, in final review |
| Raayl connection points | How the rail talks to Raayl's ledger, payments and wallets without changing Raayl's code | One blocking issue left, being fixed |
| Client transaction history | Records every step, failure, retry and operator action under the client's ID; append-only | Two blocking issues, being fixed |
| Bank payout partner | Placeholder connection for paying out to a bank account; bank details are encrypted and never leave the module | Two blocking issues, being fixed |
| Operator case queue | Puts every exception in front of a person (requotes, under- and overpayments, stuck payouts), with client consent and two people for write-offs | In first review |
| Design changes from Raayl's answers | Updates the design for Raayl's manual OTC desk, case queue, fiat pay-in and history | Reviewed and fixed once; two issues remain, to be closed before final sign-off |
